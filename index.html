01 · Executive Summary
The Offering
7 Units · Boyle Heights

Marcus & Millichap is pleased to present 3551–3559 Siskiyou Street, a seven-unit Tudor-style apartment community in the heart of Boyle Heights, offered free-and-clear at $1,100,000.

The Property comprises six separate bungalow-style structures totaling ±5,302 rentable square feet on a generous ±11,225-square-foot lot. The unit mix is all two-bedroom — six 2BD/1BA homes and one 2BD/1.5BA — including two brand-new (2023) accessory dwelling units that add modern, fully-permitted income to a vintage 1929 asset.

At the offered price the Property reflects a 6.20% current cap rate on in-place income and an 8.56 GRM, with a clear, near-term path to a 10.31% stabilized cap rate as three legacy units — currently renting at $560–$641 versus a $2,100 market — are brought to market on natural turnover. The going-in basis of $157,143 per unit and $207 per square foot sits well below replacement cost and recent Boyle Heights trades.

The Value-Add Thesis

The three legacy units rent ~70% below market. Bringing the rent roll to market grows scheduled rent +38% and lifts NOI from $68,149 to $113,421 (+66%) — and the underwritten $2,100 rent is itself below the $2,360–$2,430 Boyle Heights 2BR average, leaving further upside.

Boyle Heights Market Overview
East Los Angeles

One of Los Angeles’ most centrally located and supply-constrained rental submarkets — minutes from Downtown, ringed by freeways, and anchored by a deep, durable renter base.

Boyle Heights sits immediately east of the Los Angeles River and Downtown LA, with direct access to the 5, 10, 60 and 101 freeways and Metro E (Gold) Line service at Indiana and Soto stations. The neighborhood’s historic, low-rise housing stock and minimal new supply support consistent occupancy and steady rent growth.

3551–3559 Siskiyou is positioned near Lorena Street and Whittier Boulevard — a primary East LA retail and transit corridor — placing tenants within easy reach of employment, services and the broader Downtown job core roughly three miles to the west.
